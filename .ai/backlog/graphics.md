| T48 | Re-sharing chunks after a full rebuild | A publication shares a chunk only when no row in it moved, and a row "moved" when its node epoch OR its fold serial changed (review 6, 2026-11-19). ### 2.4 Object-model leftovers

| # | Item | What | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| T15 | Device independence of the SDK's shared mechanisms | **ONE Vulkan semantic is left, and it is over-specified rather than misspelled.** Two of the three are gone: `VkFormat` became the opaque `DeviceFormat` (`DrawState.hpp:123`, `kUndefined = 0`) and `TargetPlan`'s subpass became the backend-computed `RenderPassKey`. What remains is `DrawState.hpp`'s own `ImageLayout` (5 values), used **70 times in 10 files** (SDK 5 / vk 38 / vsg 27). D3D12 maps it one-to-one (`D3D12_RESOURCE_STATE_*`); Metal and WebGPU have no layouts at all, so a backend there could only IGNORE the declaration and derive barriers itself - the cost is not a mapping table but that "who transitions" moves out of the declaration and into the backend. **No new Vulkan spelling is added before the trigger**, and the rule has an execution point: `scripts/check_vk_exposure.py` (the SDK includes no Vulkan header and exposes no `Vk*` type; `ImageLayout` passes because the SDK declares it, and that gate's own rationale names it as the allowed Vulkan SEMANTICS). Retiring it means both backends derive barriers from pass structure + usage (vk `BarrierPlan`, vsg `TargetObjects` / `ClearPlan`) - a refactor with no consumer today. **T30** asks the sibling question about TYPES: same trigger, different subject, so the two stay separate entries and are read together when either fires | a backend of a second **API family** (D3D12 / Metal / WebGPU) appears; hand-written Vulkan is the same family and does not trigger |
